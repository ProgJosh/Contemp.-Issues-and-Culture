01 / The project

A group Q&A about AI, fashion and the people behind the pixels.

For BSM24005, our group explored a contemporary issue shaping the creative industries: how AI is changing fashion design, creative practice and employment. We built a six-slide Q&A presentation and recorded a group response.

My role was Business Research. I investigated how AI affects jobs, skills and business models, then worked with the team to shape the argument and choose sources that supported it.

  • Module Contemporary Issues and Culture in Creative Industries
  • Group Kadisha, Marie, Madalina, Grace & Sylvester
  • Output Six-slide Q&A and group recording
  • Focus AI as a creative tool in the fashion industry

03 / Reflection

What I’ll carry forward.

150-word reflection · Task A3

This module showed me how important employability skills are, not just subject knowledge. Working in a group made me manage my time, focus on priorities, and be someone my team could count on. Researching new topics also helped me follow instructions and stay open to learning different viewpoints. I realized that sticking to my commitments doesn’t just affect me; it also impacts my group’s progress. When I’m unsure about something, I now know to ask questions and pay attention to feedback, instead of ignoring problems. These experiences matter for my future career because jobs need people who are organized, reliable, and able to work with others. Next time, I plan to start tasks earlier, watch deadlines, talk more with my team, and keep track of my work. For example, during one project, I struggled with time management but learned to adjust my schedule. I’ll use lessons like this to keep improving in the future.

Next steps

  • 01 Start tasks earlier
  • 02 Watch deadlines
  • 03 Talk more with my team
